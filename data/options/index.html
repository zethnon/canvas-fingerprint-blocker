Canvas protection

Checking registration…

Reload open tabs after installing, changing settings or granting permission. The R badge is a reload reminder, not a count of trackers or proof a page is protected.

Exceptions

Comma-separated hostnames. Subdomains are included; IP addresses match exactly. Reload affected tabs after saving. IPv6 exceptions are not supported.

Coverage

Canvas and main-thread OffscreenCanvas readbacks are perturbed. Opaque/fallback frames may block reads instead. Worker canvases, WebGL readPixels, audio and fonts are not covered. This does not provide anonymity or prevent cookie/IP tracking.

Colours are projected into site-shifted four-value buckets. Image-processing and HDR workflows may need an exception.
